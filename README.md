🧠 TruthLens

An AI-powered truth verification system that detects misinformation, scams, and manipulated content across text, links, and media in real time.

🚀 Overview

TruthLens is a unified AI platform designed to combat the growing problem of misinformation in the digital world. It analyzes text claims, URLs, images, and multimedia content to determine their credibility and provide clear, explainable insights to users.

Instead of just labeling something as “true” or “false”, TruthLens explains why — making information verification transparent, understandable, and accessible.

🎯 Problem Statement

The internet is flooded with fake news, scam links, and manipulated media. Users often lack a reliable, unified system to verify information across different formats.

Existing solutions are fragmented and lack explainability, leading to confusion and misinformation spread.

💡 Solution

TruthLens solves this by providing a single AI-powered verification system that:

🧾 Fact-checks text-based claims

🔗 Analyzes URLs for safety and credibility

🖼️ Detects suspicious or manipulated media (deepfake indicators)

🧠 Explains results in simple human-readable form

👥 Integrates community-driven reporting

⚙️ Features

🤖 AI Assistant (Ai-Shafi)

A conversational AI that assists users naturally and switches to analysis mode when needed.

🧾 Fact Verification

Checks factual claims against known patterns and evidence-based reasoning.

🔗 Link Analysis

Evaluates websites and URLs for trustworthiness and potential risk indicators.

🖼️ Media Authenticity Detection

Analyzes images for manipulation, inconsistencies, or deepfake-like patterns.

🔥 Viral Misinformation Detection (Optional Mode)

Detects emotionally manipulated or viral misleading content patterns.

👥 Community Feed

Users can share, report, and flag suspicious content for collective awareness.

🧠 Key Innovation

Unified verification across text, links, and media
AI explanations instead of black-box answers
Hybrid approach: AI + community validation
Real-world focus on misinformation and digital safety
